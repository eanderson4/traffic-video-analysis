**Title:** The highD Dataset: A Drone Dataset of Naturalistic Vehicle Trajectories on German Highways for Validation of Highly Automated Driving Systems  

**Abstract key points:**  
- Scenario-based testing for highly automated vehicles relies on real-world data.  
- Current measurement methods fail to meet all requirements (reasonable effort, naturalistic behavior, sufficient quality).  
- Proposes a novel aerial (drone) measurement method.  
- Provides a large-scale naturalistic vehicle trajectory dataset called **highD** from German highways.  

**Dataset statistics (exact figures):**  
- 16.5 hours of measurements  
- 6 locations  
- 110,000 vehicles  
- Total driven distance: 45,000 km  
- 5,600 recorded complete lane changes
