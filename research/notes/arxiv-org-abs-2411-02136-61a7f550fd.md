### Abstract Fact Extraction

- **Location:** Songdo International Business District, South Korea  
- **Experiment:** Multi-drone experiment covering **20 intersections**  
- **Data captured:** Approximately **12 TB of 4K video data** over **four days**  
- **Datasets produced:**  
  1. **Songdo Traffic dataset** – ~700,000 unique vehicle trajectories  
  2. **Songdo Vision dataset** – >5,000 human-annotated images with ~300,000 vehicle instances in **4 classes**  
- **Validation:** Compared with high-precision sensor data from an instrumented probe vehicle  
- **Contributions summarized:**  
  - Tailored object detector for high-altitude bird’s-eye view  
  - Track stabilization method using detected vehicle bounding boxes as exclusion masks during image registration  
  - Orthophoto and master frame-based georeferencing strategy for consistent multi-viewpoint alignment  
  - Robust vehicle dimension estimation and detailed road segmentation  
- **Public release:** Both Songdo Traffic and Songdo Vision datasets, plus complete source code for the extraction pipeline  
- **Claims:** Establishes new benchmarks in data quality, reproducibility, and scalability for traffic research
