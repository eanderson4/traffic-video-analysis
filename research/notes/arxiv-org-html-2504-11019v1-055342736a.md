### Dataset Overview

- **Name:** DRIFT (Drone-derived Intelligence For Traffic analysis)
- **Location:** Daejeon, South Korea. Corridor from 99 Daehak-ro (Chungnam National University Five-Way Intersection) to 291 Daehak-ro (KAIST). ~2.6 km continuous roadway.
- **Coverage:** Nine interconnected intersections (Sites A–I), each with average 6 lanes.
- **Collection period:** Morning peak hours 7:30 AM – 9:20 AM.
- **Drone altitude:** ~250 meters.
- **Total raw video:** ~0.6 TB.
- **Synchronized recording duration:** 51 minutes 43 seconds (three flights: 14:02, 20:29, 17:12). 10-minute battery breaks between flights.
- **Total vehicle trajectories:** 81,699.
  - Class breakdown: Bus 2,555, Car 70,255, Truck 10,889.
- **Spatial coverage claimed:** 2.3 km (text says 2.3 km in Table 1, but body says 2.6 km – discrepancy noted).
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840×2160 pixels) at ~30 fps.

### Object Detection & Tracking

- **Detection model:** YOLOv11m (Ultralytics)
- **Bounding box method:** Oriented Bounding Box (OBB) – polygon-based, four corner points, rotation angle θ, center (cx,cy), width w, height h.
- **Tracking algorithm:** ByteTracker
- **Target classes:** Car, Bus, Truck (class IDs: 1=bus, 2=car, 3=truck)
- **Annotations:** Manually annotated polygon format. 2,301 video frames, 292,570 object instances.
- **Data split:** Training 233,944 instances, Validation 29,478, Test 29,148.
- **Hyperparameters:** Batch size 4, learning rate 0.01, IoU threshold 0.7, 500 epochs, early stopping.

### Preprocessing Pipeline

- **Orthophoto generation:** GCPs measured with EMLID RS2+ GNSS receiver. Agisoft Metashape used to generate orthophoto.
- **Frame stabilization:** ORB feature detection + Brute-Force Matcher + MAGSAC++ transformation matrix. Fallback: GeoAlign heuristic (scale + rotation + translation).
- **Lane RoIs:** Polygon defined for each lane at each site; lane-specific codes assigned.
- **Coordinate system:** Homography-based mapping to real-world coordinates via orthophoto.

### Example Findings (Section 5)

- **Lane changes:** Concentrated around merging zones.
- **Time-to-Collision (TTC):** ~10–15% of values below 2 seconds; average TTC ~5 seconds across sites. Low TTC predominately in merging sections.
- **Flow-density (Site I):** Higher flow in underpass zones (B,C,K,J); lower flow near right-turn and U-turn zones.
- **Time-space diagram (Site G):** Eastbound stable speeds; westbound repeated slowdowns and queue formation near signals – directional asymmetry.
- **Speed heatmap:** Grid-based lane-level speeds across A–I.
