## Source Notes: ArXiv 2404.17212 – “Scrutinizing Data from Sky: An Examination of Its Veracity in Area Based Traffic Contexts”

- **Title:** Scrutinizing Data from Sky: An Examination of Its Veracity in Area Based Traffic Contexts  
- **Authors:** Yawar Ali (1), Krishnan K N (1), Debashis Ray Sarkar (1), K. Ramachandra Rao (1), Niladri Chatterjee (1), Ashish Bhaskar (2)  
  - (1) Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, New Delhi, India

### Tool Under Study
- **Data from Sky (DFS):** Commercial image-processing and AI tool for traffic data extraction.  
- **Company claim:** 98% to 100% accuracy.

### Study Objective
- Validate DFS in heterogeneous, area-based (non-lane) traffic typical of developing countries.  
- Used four datasets with varied camera angles/heights in Delhi-NCR, India.

### Data Collection Specifications

| Dataset | Tool | View Angle (deg) | Height (m) | Duration (min) | Traffic Directions |
|---------|------|------------------|------------|----------------|-------------------|
| 1 (bird’s-eye) | UAV (DJI Mini 2) | 90° (bird’s-eye) | 150 | 30 | East/West (renamed NB/SB) |
| 2 (angled) | UAV (DJI Mini 2) | 60° | 50 | 30 | North/South |
| 3 (angled) | UAV (DJI Mini 2) | 60° | 25 | 30 | North/South |
| 4 (mounted) | GoPro Hero 7 | 30° | 5.5 (FoB) | 30 | North/South |

- **UAV:** DJI Mini 2, 4K resolution, 30 fps.  
- **Mounted camera:** GoPro Hero 7, 4K, 25 fps.  
- **UAV battery backup:** 15–20 minutes (causes gaps in continuous recording).  
- **Video stitching:** iMovies software.  
- **Trap lengths for SMS calculation:** 62 m (bird’s-eye), 100 m (angled 50 m), 47 m (angled 25 m), 32 m (mounted camera).

### Manual Extraction
- **Classified Volume Count (CVC):** Gate method; vehicle classes: M2W, M3W, Cars, LCV, Bus, HCV.  
- **Space Mean Speed (SMS):** Two gates with known trap length; entry/exit timestamps for random vehicles (100 per class, or full population for rare classes like Bus).

### DFS Extraction
- Upload stitched video to DFS website (paid service).  
- Download processed output file, viewable only in proprietary DFS viewer.  
- CVC: via “Export Traffic Analysis to Excel” and gate crossing events.  
- SMS: requires geo-registration (calibration using real-world coordinates) and annotation of entry/exit gates.  
- DFS viewer manual: <https://intercom.help/datafromsky/en/collections/1997337-trafficsurvey-post-recording-processing-of-videos>

### Results – CVC (Table 2)
- **Percentage error (e_p) formula:** (Manual – DFS) / Manual × 100. Negative = false positives; positive = false negatives.
- **Bird’s-eye (90°, 150 m):** Low errors overall.  
  - Example: M2W NB: Manual 336, DFS 349, e_p = −3.9%; SB: Manual 356, DFS 336, e_p = 5.6%.  
  - Bus: 0% error both directions.  
  - HCV: NB −6.5%, SB −4.8%.

- **Angled 50 m–60°:** High errors for Bus and HCV.  
  - Bus NB: Manual 39, DFS 48, e_p = −23.1%; SB: Manual 28, DFS 51, e_p = −82.1% (false positives).  
  - HCV NB: Manual 51, DFS 21, e_p = 58.8% (false negatives); SB: Manual 80, DFS 57, e_p = 28.8%.

- **Angled 25 m–60°:** Bus error resolved (0% NB), but HCV error extreme:  
  - HCV SB: Manual 31, DFS 92, e_p = −197% (false positives due to LCV misclassification in congested NB direction).

- **Mounted camera (5.5 m, 30°):** Moderate errors.  
  - Example: Cars NB: Manual 1530, DFS 1598, e_p = −4.4%; SB: Manual 1343, DFS 1391, e_p = −3.6%.  

- **Key observation:** Misclassification occurs between similar-sized vehicles (M3W↔LCV, Bus↔HCV) depending on direction (moving toward/away from camera) and traffic state (congested vs. free-flow).

### Results – SMS (Table 3)
- **Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) for each vehicle class and data set.**  
- **All MAPE values < 10%** (considered good).  
- **Best (lowest) MAPE:** Bird’s-eye view (e.g., M2W NB 1.92%, SB 3.49%; Cars NB 2.61%, SB 1.80%).  
- **Highest MAPE:** Mounted camera NB direction (M2W 7.11%, M3W 7.07%, LCV 7.13%, Bus 4.80%, HCV 6.90%).  
- **Angled 25 m** generally better than angled 50 m (tradeoff: height vs. coverage).  
- SB (South Bound, moving toward camera) often has higher MAPE in 50 m angled data due to congestion.

### Microscopic Trajectory Validation
- **Probe vehicle:** GPS trajectories recorded with **Geo Tracker** (Android app) in KML format.  
- **Simultaneous drone capture:** Bird’s-eye at 60 m height.  
- **DFS extraction:** After geo-registration, identified probe vehicle ID and extracted trajectory.  
- **Validation methods:**  
  - Subjective: visual comparison of speed vs. time plots (10 samples) – “only marginal errors”.  
  - Objective: paired t-test (α = 0.05).  
    - **Pearson correlation:** 0.94 (very strong positive linear relationship).  
    - **p-value (two-tailed):** 0.42 (> α), null hypothesis (equal means) accepted → no significant difference between DFS and GPS speeds.

### Conclusions
- DFS bird’s eye view yields accurate results in heterogeneous, area-based traffic (MAPE < 4% for SMS; CVC errors low).  
- Angled data accuracy is affected by camera angle, height, traffic direction, and traffic state (congestion).  
- DFS is not well-trained for highly heterogeneous traffic when camera is angled.  
- Microscopic trajectories validated subjectively and objectively, provided geo-registration is precise.  
- **Limitations:** Not tested for nighttime, adverse wind, foggy weather; only midblock locations (no intersections/roundabouts).
