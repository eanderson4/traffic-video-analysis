**Key concepts:**
- **pNEUMA experiment:** Large-scale urban traffic dataset using a swarm of drones.
- **pNEUMA Vision:** Extended dataset that adds imagery (aerial video) and bounding box annotations for vehicles, plus new features such as vehicle azimuth.
- **Anomaly detection:** Novel method using regularized anomaly detection to denoise vision-extracted trajectories. Distinguishes stationary vs. non-stationary errors – claims non-stationary errors account for the largest part of noise.
- **Key result:** After denoising, lateral acceleration of motorcycles is significantly larger than that of other vehicle types.
- **Density map estimation:** Using image segmentation to estimate number of vehicles from drone images; achieves comparable results to detection-based counting. Time–space diagrams from density maps identified congested road queues better than a widely used vehicle detection method.

**Methods mentioned:**
- Computer vision methods for vehicle counting: density map vs. detection.
