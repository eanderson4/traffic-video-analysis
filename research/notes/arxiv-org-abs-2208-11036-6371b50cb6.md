### Dataset Overview
- **Size:** 1,140 minutes of drone videos.
- **Locations:** 12 recording sites.
- **Road geometries covered:** Freeway basic segments, signalized intersections, stop-controlled intersections, control-free intersections.
- **Trajectory generation:** Five-step procedure:
  1. Video stabilization
  2. Object filtering
  3. Multi-video stitching
  4. Object detection and tracking
  5. Enhanced error filtering
- **Key feature:** Provides rotated bounding box information for vehicles, demonstrated to improve safety evaluations.
- **Safety-critical events included:** Cut-in, merge, and diverge events – validated by distributions of minimum time-to-collision (TTC) and minimum post-encroachment time (PET).
- **Digital twin assets:** Three-dimensional base maps of recording locations and signal timing information.

### Comparisons to Other Datasets
The abstract states that CitySim outperforms other video-based datasets in capturing safety-critical events, but does not explicitly name comparators (e.g., NGSIM, highD). The claim is based on distributions of TTC and PET.
