### Dataset Details (from abstract)
- **Total drone video recorded**: 1140 minutes (19 hours)
- **Number of recording locations**: 12
- **Road geometries covered**: freeway basic segments, signalized intersections, stop-controlled intersections, control-free intersections
- **Trajectory extraction procedure**: five-step pipeline:
  1. Video stabilization
  2. Object filtering
  3. Multi-video stitching
  4. Object detection and tracking
  5. Enhanced error filtering
- **Additional data**: rotated bounding boxes for vehicles; 3D base maps of recording locations; signal timings
- **Safety validation**: critical events (cut-in, merge, diverge) validated using distributions of minimum time-to-collision (TTC) and minimum post-encroachment time (PET)
- **Claim**: Rotated bounding boxes improve safety evaluations.
- **Purpose**: Facilitate safety-oriented research and digital twin applications.
